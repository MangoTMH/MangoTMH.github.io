PERSONAL PROJECT / AI-ASSISTED RESEARCH

V1 · In progress

Trading Research Agents

An AI-assisted research and decision-support framework I’m developing to make discretionary trading more structured, evidence-based and measurable.

V1 is currently a conversational research framework. My focus is designing the research workflow, planning rules and review process; coded analytics and automation are future work.

  • Freeze the decision before the outcome. Record the thesis, entry conditions, invalidation, targets and risks before a trade, preserving the original plan for an honest review.
  • Separate the system from execution. Compare the V1 System Portfolio with the Actual User Portfolio to distinguish research weaknesses from sizing, contract selection and trade management.
  • Review the process, not just the profit. Evaluate thesis, setup, entry, risk planning and execution discipline—even when the outcome looks successful.
Research workflow designAI-assisted analysisForward testing
Inside the V1 framework +

Daily Market Scout

Combine market regime and breadth analysis with company research, catalysts, technical structure and options data. Candidates progress through Watch, Research Candidate, Near Trigger, Frozen V1, Open and Closed. Finding no suitable trade is a valid result.

A ledger built for learning

A structured master ledger and dashboards track realized P&L, monthly results, open positions, execution differences and process reviews. Retrospective reconstructions stay separate from prospective V1 trades so hindsight does not contaminate forward testing.

Next steps

Develop coded analytics, backtesting, structured databases and automated research pipelines while preserving point-in-time records. V1 is an early-stage experiment; a repeatable trading edge has not yet been established.
